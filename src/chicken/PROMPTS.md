# ChatGPT image prompts: TaxSMP "Chicken" tool set

Paste these into ONE ChatGPT chat, in order. Start with prompt 0 so the
style is locked in, then do 1–4. If a result looks off (blurry, wrong shape,
fake pixels), say "regenerate, stricter pixel grid" or just run it again.

Save the images in this folder as:
`set.png`, `sell_axe.png`, `tree_chopper.png`, `drill.png`, `shovel.png`

These are only references. Claude redraws each one by hand on a real
32x32 grid, so it doesn't matter if ChatGPT's pixels aren't perfect.
What matters is the shape, the colors and where the details go.

```

  ice blue: #F0FFFF #A8F0FF #4CD3F5 #1E8FCB #0D4C7E

- Flat solid magenta #FF00FF background. Nothing else behind the items.

THE FOUR ITEMS:
- Top-left, SELL AXE: single-bladed axe. Dark handle with two gold bands and a gold pommel. Broad crescent axe head of polished gold with a bright ice-blue cutting edge. A round gold coin set into the middle of the head with a dark-gold dollar sign on it. A small ice-blue gem on top of the socket.

- Bottom-left, POWER DRILL: cordless pistol-grip drill seen from the side. Grip points down-left, drill bit points up-right. Dark netherite body with gold accent plates, gold trigger, a glowing ice-blue battery at the base of the grip and a small ice-blue light on the body. Gold chuck. Long gold spiral twist bit with an ice-blue tip, the spiral shown as alternating diagonal light and dark stripes.
- Bottom-right, SHOVEL: heavy shovel. Dark handle with gold bands and a D-shaped gold grip at the bottom-left end. Wide pointed spade blade of polished gold at the top-right with a bright ice-blue edge, and a faceted ice-blue crystal at the neck where the blade meets the shaft.
```
